## How to read this

Every item carries a source tag and a status.

| Tag | Means |
|---|---|
| `[WP p.N]` | Welcome pack page N |
| `[YT id]` | YouTube video id (youtube.com/watch?v=id) |
| `[X acct/id]` | x.com/acct/status/id |

| Status | Means | Bot and site may use it? |
|---|---|---|
| **RECORD** | A public, timestamped post, filing or document anyone can check | Yes, with the link |
| **READ** | The community's interpretation of a record | Yes, framed as a read ("the community reads it as…") |
| **CLAIM** | Something the project, the welcome pack or a video asserts but nobody has proven | Only quoted and attributed, never as fact |
| **MARKET** | A historical price, market cap or volume | No. Bot never posts price talk. Kept for context only |
| **OFF-LIMITS** | See section 13 | Never |
| `[verify]` | Not yet checked against the primary source | Not until checked |

Times are US Eastern (ET) unless marked.
